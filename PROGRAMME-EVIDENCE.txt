  with no block ledger, no state.days and no logged sessions, so it measures
  ONE thing: can the volume arithmetic run away. It cannot see the
  progression gate at all -- progressionJustification() reads the block that
  just ended, and no block ends here -- so every athlete below grows
  identically and every column converges on the backstop. That convergence is
  the arithmetic bound holding, not a programme an athlete would be given.
  For what five different athletes are actually prescribed, run
  tools/shots/trajectories.js.
