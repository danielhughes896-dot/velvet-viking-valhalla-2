SACORO is a running-training app. This page explains exactly what leaves your device, when, and why: no more and no less than that.

The short version

  • SACORO needs an account. You sign in with a link we email you, so there is no password for you to remember.
  • Your email address and your training plan and log are stored in your account with our database provider, and nowhere else, so your plan is backed up and follows you to another device. A working copy is also kept on your own device.
  • Strava sync is switched off for this private beta, so no Strava data is read or stored for any beta tester. When it is available, it reads your recent running activities to help log your training. It never writes anything back to Strava, and never sees your Strava password.
  • We don't run ads, don't sell data, and don't use tracking or analytics scripts.
  • SACORO's AI coach uses Anthropic's API. To write about a run or a week, it is given your logged figures for that run or week (distance, pace, time and effort) and SACORO's own conclusions about them. Your heart rate is only sent if you have given health-data consent. Notes you write yourself are never sent, and nothing that came from Strava is ever sent. If you ask your coach a question, what you type is sent to Anthropic too, so it can answer you.
  • You can delete your account and everything we hold on our servers at any time from Settings, or by asking us. The copy on your own phone or browser is removed by you.

What's stored on your device

The app keeps a working copy of your training in your browser's local storage on your own device: your plan, your logged workouts, your notes, your preferences. That is what lets it open and work without a signal. A backup file only leaves your device if you export one yourself.

What's stored in your account

SACORO needs an account. You sign in with a link sent to your email, so there is no password for you to create or remember. The following is stored with our database provider, Supabase:

DataWhy

This data is protected so that only your own signed-in session can read it: not other users, and not us browsing the database casually. Supabase hosts this data on our behalf as our data processor; Vercel hosts the app itself and the small server functions that talk to Supabase, Anthropic and Strava on your behalf, so tokens and credentials never reach your device.

The AI coach

SACORO's AI coach uses Anthropic's API to explain and discuss your training with you. It does not decide anything. SACORO's own engine works out your plan, your paces and your progression, and the AI coach only explains and discusses what has already been decided. It never writes to your plan.

To write the analysis for a run or a week, the AI coach is given your logged figures for that run or week (distance, pace, time and effort) and SACORO's own conclusions about them. Your heart rate is only included if you have given health-data consent in Settings. Notes you write yourself are never sent, and nothing that reached SACORO from Strava is ever sent, even if you have connected Strava. The analysis it writes is saved with your plan in your account, the same as anything else SACORO writes about your training.

If you ask your coach a question, what you type is sent to Anthropic too, along with the same kind of training information, so it can answer you. The same rules apply.

Coach Memory is off until you turn it on in Settings. With it on, your coach keeps short one-line notes in your account: what you asked it, and choices such as turning down an extra run. The most recent few are given to the AI coach as background with each question. Nothing from Strava is ever kept, and pain, illness and other health details are kept only if you have agreed to health data use. You can read or clear the notes in Settings at any time, and they are deleted with your account.

What's stored if you connect Strava

Not available during the private beta. Strava sync is switched off for this beta while we confirm approval with Strava, so no Strava data is read, received or stored for any beta tester. The Connect control is not offered, and the server refuses a connection even if the app is bypassed. This section describes how it works when it is switched back on.

Connecting Strava is optional. If you connect it:

  • Your Strava sign-in tokens are stored server-side only, protected so that no browser, including your own, can read them back out. They exist purely so the server can fetch your activities on your behalf.
  • From each running activity, we read and store: distance, duration, pace, heart rate, cadence, and elevation gain. We do not store GPS routes or maps, photos, or any Strava social data (kudos, comments, followers).
  • The connection is read-only. SACORO cannot post, edit, or delete anything in your Strava account, and never requests permission to.
  • You can disconnect Strava at any time from Settings. Disconnecting stops any further access and deletes the copy of your Strava activity data we hold server-side. It isn't kept around after you disconnect. Values already merged into your own training log (distance, pace, heart rate and so on, logged against a specific day in your plan) are your record of your own run and stay in your log, the same as anything you type in yourself. Disconnecting Strava doesn't reach back and strip them out.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

On our servers. Your account and stored plan, including any analysis your AI coach has written and any Coach Memory notes, are kept until the account is deleted. You can do that yourself at any time from Settings, and it removes your email, your stored plan, your Coach Memory notes, your Strava connection and any staged Strava activity data in one action. If you are a beta tester and would rather just ask, email support@velvetviking.co.uk and we will delete your account and all identifiable personal and training data Velvet Viking holds on its servers within 30 days of receiving your request.

On your own device. This part we cannot reach, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise. Your plan, your logged runs and your saved sign-in are stored by the app on your own phone or browser. Deleting your account does not erase them, and neither does anything we can do from our side. They stay under your control: Reset Plan in Settings clears the training plan itself, and clearing the app's or browser's stored data, or uninstalling the app, removes everything the app has kept locally, including any plan parked on a shared device and your stored sign-in.

If your phone backs its apps up to your own cloud account (Android's own backup, for example) a copy may exist there too. That backup belongs to you and your device maker, not to Velvet Viking, and we have no way to reach into it. Your device's backup settings are where that is managed.

Your rights

  • Access: export a full copy of your data at any time from Settings.
  • Correction: edit your plan, logs, and settings directly in the app.
  • Deletion: delete your account and everything stored on our servers, from Settings, or by asking us at support@velvetviking.co.uk. Copies on your own device are removed by you, as described above.
