Velvet Viking

Pace-zoned training blocks for anything from a 5K to a 50K ultra — built around your race date, your weekly mileage and a recent benchmark time.

Install on Android

Download the app

Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser — that's expected, it just means the app doesn't come from the Play Store. Tap allow, then open the downloaded file.

Add to your iPhone

Add the app itself, not this page — otherwise you'll get a shortcut back to these instructions.

  1. Tap Open the app below
  2. In Safari, tap the Share button
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen
Open the app

You'll get the gold medallion icon and a full-screen app with no browser bars — that is the iPhone version. Apple only allows real installs through the App Store, so this is the way in on iOS.

Open in your browser

Launch the app

Works on any device. Your plan is saved on this device.

Just open it in this browser instead

Your training plan is stored privately on your own device by default. An account may be needed to use Velvet Viking — signing in also backs your plan up and lets you use it on more than one device, and your plan and email are then securely stored with your Velvet Viking account. You can also back up any time from Settings → Export Full Backup.
