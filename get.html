Install on Android

Download the app

Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser — that's expected, it just means the app doesn't come from the Play Store. Tap allow, then open the downloaded file.

Add to your iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen

You'll get a full-screen icon that works just like an installed app. There's no iPhone download — Apple only allows installs through the App Store.
